Main Characters

1. Frieren

Elven Mage

An ancient elven mage who was a member of the original Hero's Party. She is quiet, curious, and collects strange magic spells.

2. Fern

Human Mage / Apprentice

A disciplined mage trained by Heiter and taken in as Frieren's traveling companion and apprentice.

3. Stark

Warrior

The apprentice of Eisen. Although timid at times, he is an amazingly strong front-line warrior.

4. Himmel

The Hero

The charismatic and kind leader of the Hero's Party who defeated the Demon King. His memory guides Frieren's journey.

5. Heiter

Priest

The corrupt priest of the original Hero Party who adopts Fern and arranges her journey with Frieren.
