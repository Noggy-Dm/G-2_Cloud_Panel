Summary of the Plot

The Demon King has been defeated, and the victorious hero party returns home before going their separate ways. For the elf mage Frieren, who lives for thousands of years, the decade-long adventure was only a fleeting moment in her vast lifetime.


Decades later, her human companions pass away due to old age. Realizing her deep regret over not getting to know them better, Frieren embarks on a new quest traveling North toward the resting place of souls, learning about humanity, friendship, and love along the way.
